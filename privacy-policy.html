Client Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Oak & Grain Interiors, Bengaluru

1. Commitment to Client Privacy

At Oak & Grain Interiors (led by Amit Singh, based in Bengaluru, Karnataka), we value the trust you place in us when sharing details regarding your home, residence, or commercial space. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, safeguard, and process your personal information when you interact with our website and consultation services.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation or estimate enquiry on our website, we collect the following information:

  • Client Name: To address you respectfully and manage your project file.
  • Mobile / Phone Number: To contact you regarding site measurements, consultation scheduling, and project discussions.
  • Email Address (Optional): To deliver detailed design presentations, project schedules, and formal quotations.
  • Service Required: To match your enquiry with the appropriate specialized design category (e.g. Modular Kitchens, Wardrobes, Living Units, or Full Home Interiors).
  • Project Details & Message: Any dimensions, apartment configurations, or aesthetic preferences you choose to share.

3. How We Use Your Enquiry Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond promptly to your design enquiries and coordinate consultations.
  • To arrange complimentary site visits and laser measurements across Bengaluru.
  • To prepare accurate cost estimates, 2D floor plans, and 3D interior renders.
  • To communicate during project execution and post-installation warranty support.

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or aggregators.

4. Database Storage & Security Architecture

All client submissions are stored in an encrypted cloud database powered by Supabase PostgreSQL. We employ strict enterprise-grade security practices:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Enforced database policies ensure that public visitors cannot read, browse, or query submitted customer enquiries.
  • Authorised Admin Access: Only pre-approved studio email accounts (Amit Singh and designated studio managers) have cryptographic credentials to view client records.
  • Encrypted Transmission: All communication between your browser and our database is secured with modern SSL/TLS encryption.

5. Website Interaction & Activity Tracking

To improve our website responsiveness and monitor interaction quality, our platform records basic interaction events (such as button clicks on Call, WhatsApp, and form submissions). Phone call tracking only records that a visitor triggered the call link; it does not access, record, or track the actual call connection, voice audio, or duration.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have full ownership of your personal information. If you wish to review, update, or permanently delete your contact records from our database, please contact Amit Singh directly at oakandgraininteriors@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 93530 19525, and we will fulfill your request promptly.
