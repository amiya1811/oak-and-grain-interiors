Oak & Grain Interiors offers complimentary initial design consultations and physical laser measurements for residences across Bengaluru. Initial discussions, moodboards, and preliminary estimates are provided in good faith to assist clients in planning their interior investments.

All project estimates generated through our website or subsequent discussions are valid for thirty (30) calendar days from issuance. Every piece of furniture, wardrobe module, and modular kitchen is manufactured to tailored dimensions in our Bengaluru workshop. Because natural wood, veneers, and stone exhibit intrinsic grain variations, subtle nuances in grain pattern and wood tone reflect authentic, organic quality and will not be considered craftsmanship defects.
